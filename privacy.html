DriveSync privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What DriveSync is

DriveSync is a personal desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own.

What data it accesses

With your permission, DriveSync uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope. This only lets it create, read and update the files it creates itself, in a folder called “DriveSync” in your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. It also reads your Google account's email address to show which account is signed in.

How the data is used

The files you choose are copied between your computers and your Google Drive, and nowhere else. DriveSync does not collect analytics, does not sell or share data, and sends nothing to anyone other than Google.

Where data is stored

Your synced files are stored in your own Google Drive and on your own computers. Your Google sign-in token and DriveSync's settings are stored only on your computer, in DriveSync's settings folder.

Removing access

You can sign out in DriveSync's Settings, or remove DriveSync's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling DriveSync and deleting its settings folder removes all data it stored on your computer. Your files in Google Drive stay yours.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DriveSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on Google's sign-in screen for DriveSync.
